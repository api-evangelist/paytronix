TL;DR: A slam menu is a pre-planned, condensed version of your regular restaurant menu that helps your team maintain speed, consistency, and profitability when order volume suddenly spikes. Keep reading to learn which items belong on a slam menu, how to know when to activate it, and how staff training and restaurant technology can make the difference between a controlled rush and an overwhelmed operation.
