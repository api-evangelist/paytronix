In the U.S., opening a cafe typically costs between $80,000 and $300,000, depending on your concept, location, and how much work the space requires before you're ready to open. A small drive-thru kiosk in a suburban strip mall and a full-service cafe in a downtown storefront both fall under the umbrella of "opening a cafe," yet their startup costs can differ by more than $200,000.
