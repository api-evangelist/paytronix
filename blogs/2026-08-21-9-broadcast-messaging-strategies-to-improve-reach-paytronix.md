When you need to send a broadcast to 10,000 people quickly, channels such as email, phone calls, and social media may not provide the immediacy or scale required. Broadcast messaging offers an alternative: one sender can dispatch a single message to multiple recipients as individual texts, allowing each recipient to receive and respond to the communication privately.
