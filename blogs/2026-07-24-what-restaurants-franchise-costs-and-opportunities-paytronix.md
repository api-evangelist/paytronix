Restaurant franchising in the United States spans more than 238,000 quick-service and full-service locations, and those sectors are projected to generate over $400 billion in revenue by 2027. The restaurant sector has seen remarkable growth over the past three decades, with industry sales roughly doubling since the turn of the millennium.
