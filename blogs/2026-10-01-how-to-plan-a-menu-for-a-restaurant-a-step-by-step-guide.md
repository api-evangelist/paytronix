TL;DR: Planning a menu is not the same as picking dishes you like. It is a structured process of concept alignment, costing, engineering, and scheduled revision that decides whether your food makes money. Most operators skip the costing step until after the menu prints, which is exactly when a margin problem becomes expensive to fix.
